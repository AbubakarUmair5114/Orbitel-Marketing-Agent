## 1. Executive Memo: The Business Problem & Strategic Solution

### The Challenge
In telecom operations across competitive markets, customer churn is the largest driver of top-line revenue destruction. Orbitel previously suffered from:
- **Delayed Escalations (48–72 Hour Lag):** Critical customer complaints (cell tower blackouts, roaming billing disputes, abusive agent interactions) lingered in unmonitored ticket queues until customers formally ported out.
- **Siloed Departmental Communication:** Field network teams, finance billing desks, and marketing retention desks had no shared real-time signal.
- **Unstructured Telemetry Invisibility:** Customer sentiment across WhatsApp, email, call transcripts, and app feedback was qualitative, unquantified, and invisible to C-Level decision-makers.

### The Delivered Solution
The **Orbitel Automated Customer Sentiment & Churn Risk Intelligence Engine** is a production-grade, closed-loop AI automation pipeline. It ingests omnichannel feedback in real time, applies client-side PII masking, extracts 33 discrete analytical variables via GPT-4o-mini, logs data idempotently to an enterprise master ledger, dispatches instant HTML escalation emails directly to department heads, and powers an executive single-page Bento Grid Power BI scorecard.
